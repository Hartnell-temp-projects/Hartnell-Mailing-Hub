Dear Panthers,

Our Information Technology Department has been made aware of a fraudulent email circulating that falsely claims to offer paid employment opportunities at Hartnell College. The message impersonates a Hartnell employee and directs students to respond using a personal Outlook email address.

Please be aware:

  • This email is not legitimate and was not sent by Hartnell College.
  • Do not reply, send your resume, or provide any personal information.
  • Do not click on any links or respond to requests for personal or financial information.

Scammers often impersonate college employees to make fraudulent job offers appear legitimate. Official Hartnell College employment opportunities are never advertised through personal email accounts and will come from authorized Hartnell communication channels.

How to protect yourself:

  • Verify the sender's email address before responding.
  • Be cautious of unsolicited job offers that promise quick or easy pay.
  • Never provide personal, banking, or financial information in response to an unexpected email.
  • If you receive this message, forward it to cybersafe@hartnell.edu and then delete the email.

Thank you for helping keep our Hartnell community safe. If you have any questions or believe you may have responded to this scam, please contact the Information Technology Department immediately at cybersafe@hartnell.edu.

Stay Cyber Safe,

Hartnell College Information Technology
